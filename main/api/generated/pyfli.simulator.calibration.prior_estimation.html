fit_beta(values, offset=0.0, scale=1.0, inflation=1.0)[source]#

Method-of-moments Beta fit on (values - offset) / scale, with the variance multiplied by inflation. The values are winsorized at their 0.5 / 99.5 percentiles (capped, not dropped), which limits single outliers without the ~3 % variance loss that trimming the tails causes.

quick_pixel_fits(fm, decays, irf_norm, bg_per_bin, is_bi, init)[source]#

Per-pixel Poisson fits with the IRF and the background fixed, parameterised like the simulator: bi -> (tau2, E, A1, N), mono -> (tau, N). Bi-exponential fits are MAP with a weak Gaussian prior around the pooled fit (BI_FIT_PRIOR_SD). They only initialise the priors; the simulation-based search refines them.

photon_prior_discrete(counts, n_active_bins, dcr, inflation=1.0, active_fraction=1.0)[source]#

Beta over n_cycles in [lo, hi] such that 0.01 * n_cycles * active_fraction matches the observed signal photons. active_fraction is the expected share of signal photons that land in active time bins (photons in bins outside the acquisition window are lost, in the data and in the windowed simulation).

photon_prior_continuous(peaks, data_max, bit, qe_on=True, inflation=1.0, scale_corr=1.0)[source]#

Beta over A / (2**bit - 1). The observed peak is A * QE with QE ~ U(0.15, 0.35) independent of A, so E[A] = E[peak] / E[QE] and E[A^2] = E[peak^2] / E[QE^2].
