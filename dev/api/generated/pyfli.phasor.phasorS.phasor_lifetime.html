normal_lifetime(g, s, frequency_hz, harmonic=1)[source]#

Normal lifetime tau_N: the lifetime of the semicircle point nearest to each phasor.

Parameters:

  • g (array_like) – IRF-calibrated phasor coordinates (any shape).

  • s (array_like) – IRF-calibrated phasor coordinates (any shape).

  • frequency_hz (float) – Laser repetition (modulation) frequency in hertz.

  • harmonic (int) – Harmonic of the phasor.

Returns:

tau_N = tan(theta / 2) / omega in nanoseconds, same shape as g: 0 at (1, 0), inf at (0, 0) (and for phasors below the axis nearest to those ends), NaN at the semicircle centre and for non-finite phasors.

Return type:

np.ndarray

apparent_lifetimes(g, s, frequency_hz, harmonic=1)[source]#

Phase, modulation and normal lifetimes of each phasor.

Parameters:

  • g (array_like) – IRF-calibrated phasor coordinates (any shape, e.g. (H, W) maps).

  • s (array_like) – IRF-calibrated phasor coordinates (any shape, e.g. (H, W) maps).

  • frequency_hz (float) – Laser repetition (modulation) frequency in hertz.

  • harmonic (int) – Harmonic of the phasor.

Returns:

{"tau_phi", "tau_m", "tau_n"} (see APPARENT_LIFETIMES), each in nanoseconds with the shape of g.

Return type:

dict[str, np.ndarray]
